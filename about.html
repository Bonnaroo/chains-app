Privacy

What we store: your username, display name, the email you sign in with, an optional recovery email and photo, the rounds you score (course, date, holes, scores, who was on your card), your bag, friends, messages, league memberships, trophies and challenge progress.

What we don't: payment details (there are none), your location history (GPS is used on the screen while you play and is not kept), contacts, or anything from other apps.

Who sees it: other signed-in players see your name, photo, title, points, course records and the scores you post at a course. Nobody outside Chains does; we don't sell or share data.

Delete it: Settings → Account → Delete my account removes your profile, trophies, friends and sign-in immediately. Scores on cards you shared with other players stay, under "Former player".

Where it lives: Google Firebase (United States). Nightly backups are kept 45 days.

Course data comes from players and open sources; nothing is copied from other apps. Questions: open an issue on GitHub. Privacy.
